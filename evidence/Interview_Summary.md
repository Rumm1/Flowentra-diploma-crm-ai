# Flowentra: Simulated Interview Summary

Task: **PD-08** | Prepared: **2026-10-24** | Evidence class: **FICTIONAL SIMULATION ONLY**

**No real interviews were conducted for this artifact.** The four cases, respondent roles, business sizes, answers, and problem ratings below were constructed with AI assistance from the task brief and the existing Flowentra design. There are **zero real respondents** in this dataset. No recruitment, consent, recordings, quotations from real people, or measured task timings exist. This document is a rehearsal of qualitative synthesis and a plan for fieldwork, not proof of user demand.

Read it alongside the [interview guide](Interview_Guide.md) and [note template](Interview_Note_Template.md). The research hypotheses remain assumptions until tested with real participants. The requested themes—setup complexity, high cost, and weak AI—were supplied in the brief, so their appearance in these cases cannot count as independent discovery.

## 1. Scope and simulation method

Four fictional cases cover a real estate agency, an online store, a consulting practice, and a development studio. Two roles are owners and two are sales managers. The scenarios deliberately include both difficulties and counterexamples to exercise the guide's questions and evidence coding. No live CRM product was tested and no real vendor is attributed a failure.

The cases model different client journeys: property viewing, order inquiry, consultation booking, and discovery-to-proposal handoff. Their team sizes and processes are assumptions for this exercise. The online store and development studio may need capabilities outside the service-business MVP; their inclusion tests scope boundaries rather than establishing that all four sectors fit Flowentra equally well.

All evidence IDs use `SIM-xx-Nxx`; they identify constructed scenario notes, not transcripts. Question IDs and H01–H08 refer to the guide. Impact scores follow the template's 0–4/U rubric and are illustrative analyst judgments. Session dates, elapsed interview timestamps, recordings, task durations, and respondent verification are **N/A** for every case.

## 4. Confirmed conclusions versus starting assumptions

### 4.1. What is actually established

Only artifact-level facts are established by inspecting this package: it contains a proposed guide, a blank note template, and four labeled fictional cases with traceable synthetic note IDs. The [project proposal](../docs/01_Project_Proposal.md) and [AI design](../docs/AI_Integration_and_Security.md) document the intended MVP and its constraints. Those are repository design decisions, not interview findings or implemented capabilities.

**Confirmed findings from real interviews: none.** The themes above are supported only inside the fictional scenarios because the scenarios were authored to explore them. They cannot be relabeled as confirmed customer pain, quotes, validation, completed fieldwork, or evidence that Flowentra will sell.

## 5. Candidate Flowentra requirements and scope decisions

These requirements are proposals derived from the synthetic themes and existing design. “MVP-aligned” means compatible with PD-01/PD-05; it does not mean implemented, independently approved, or validated by users. Acceptance checks below are planned evidence, not completed tests.

| ID | Candidate requirement and rationale | Traceability | Scope disposition | Proposed acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R01 | Provide a guided first client, deal, task, and appointment journey with clear defaults and understandable fields | T01; H01/H06; SIM-01-N01, SIM-03-N01 | MVP-aligned UX direction; no custom workflow builder implied | Novice participants complete the journey; record errors, requests for help, setup steps, and actual duration before setting a usability threshold |
| R02 | Keep next actions and relevant client context easy to retrieve within the actor's permissions | T04; H03/H08; SIM-03-N03, SIM-04-N02 | MVP-aligned clients/tasks/lookup; no order/project-system synchronization implied | A permitted employee can find assigned follow-up; requests for another employee's restricted records are denied |
| R03 | Make AI assist a bounded task end to end: authorized lookup/summary and a validated task or appointment proposal | T03; H04; SIM-01-N03, SIM-03-N04 | Matches PD-05 intents; unattended execution, deal updates, and outbound messages remain excluded | Use the existing PD-05 synthetic evaluation plan; check supported, ambiguous, and unsupported prompts without claiming a measured improvement |
| R04 | Preview the resolved client, employee, action details, and relevant time/timezone before explicit confirmation | T05; H05/H07; SIM-01-N03, SIM-04-N04 | MVP-aligned confirmation; permission, current-state, and booking checks remain server-owned | No mutation before confirmation; a stale/conflicting/unauthorized proposal fails safely; manual forms remain usable during provider failure |
| R05 | Make future total cost and AI allowances understandable for a small team | T02; H02; SIM-02-N01, SIM-03-N02 | Commercial research direction; no tariff, free tier, or price advantage established | Document hosting/model/support cost for a stated workload, then test package comprehension and real purchasing trade-offs |
| R06 | Use explicit timezone/date presentation and research the needed interface/assistant languages | H07; SIM-04-N04 | Timezone handling is in the design; language delivery and local billing require further decisions | Test actual date/time entry and cross-timezone preview with intended users; record unsupported languages and outcomes |
| R07 | Evaluate migration and channel/order/project integration as possible adoption blockers | T04; H03/H08; SIM-02-N02, SIM-03-N01, SIM-04-N02 | Deferred discovery: bulk import, messengers, inventory, and external project integrations are outside MVP | Validate whether each segment can get value without the missing capability before selecting it for a pilot |
| R08 | Retain ordinary forms and a small core; do not promise to replace every specialist system | T01/T04; H06/H08; SIM-03-N04, SIM-04-N01 | Consistent with a single-organization, bounded prototype; forecasts, billing, and full help desk remain excluded | Demonstrate manual core flows and document unsupported scenarios explicitly; reconcile future scope changes through separate review |

**Provisional order of work:** retain the already designed core workflow and controlled AI boundary; investigate R01–R04 with real users; evaluate cost and localization under R05–R06; use R07 to decide segment fit before expanding scope. This ordering is a researcher's design judgment, not a result of customer votes or measured demand.

## 6. Limitations and fieldwork plan

The main limitation is complete absence of empirical participants. Additional limitations are prompt-driven theme selection, constructed behaviors, assumed team sizes, no direct observation, no measured time/cost, no purchasing commitments, and no independent coding review. The exercise cannot establish causal benefits, market prevalence, competitor performance, willingness to pay, or saturation. Generated notes are not original recordings that a reviewer could audit.

For actual research:

1. Recruit an initial wave of owners and sales managers with varied CRM satisfaction and experience, including AI non-users. Record recruitment/selection bias and seek disconfirming cases; four is a starting plan, not a sufficiency guarantee.
2. Obtain actual permission and agree private handling before collecting notes or recordings. Use the guide's neutral questions and one recent episode per participant. Introduce the concept only after unaided workflow questions.
3. Create separate real records using `Rxx` IDs and the note template. Record actual timestamps, time-estimate basis, evidence IDs, negative cases, unknowns, and severity rationale. Do not reuse SIM notes as respondent answers.
4. Have another researcher check the claim-to-evidence mapping and scope implications. Keep self-reports, observation, researcher interpretation, and prompted preference distinct.
5. Publish a separately labeled empirical synthesis containing only permitted anonymized evidence. Preserve this simulation as a rehearsal; do not overwrite its provenance or retroactively rename its cases as real participants.
6. Revisit the segment and requirements using actual findings. Record unresolved questions and failed assumptions before conducting a prototype trial or pricing experiment. Do not claim the task has independent research approval merely because its documents were committed.
