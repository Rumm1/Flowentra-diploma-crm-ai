## 5. Problem log and criticality

### Impact rubric

Use the highest justified consequence for the specific case, with evidence. These are research coding rules, not a numerical estimate of market importance.

| Score | Label | Consequence required to justify the score |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | No problem in this case | Participant explicitly reports an adequate workflow; do not use for unanswered questions |
| 1 | Minor | Small inconvenience; task completes without a substantial workaround |
| 2 | Moderate | Repeated manual workaround or recoverable delay; task still completes |
| 3 | High | Material disruption to follow-up or work delivery, or an essential capability is abandoned; consequence is described |
| 4 | Critical | Core operation cannot proceed or a serious irreversible consequence is credibly evidenced; discomfort alone is insufficient |
| U | Unknown | Consequence not established or question not covered |

Do not infer lost revenue from a delayed follow-up. Severity in a simulation is only an illustrative analyst rating, never a real participant assessment.
